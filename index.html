Why some browsers ignore "Choose Photo" on Add to Home Screen:

Many mobile browsers (especially iOS Safari when viewing preview frames, sandboxed iframes, or file:// pages) ignore JavaScript-updated apple-touch-icon tags after load or take a page snapshot instead.

Workaround: Tap Save Icon Image to save the square 180×180 photo to your Photos, add the page to your Home Screen, then long-press the Home Screen icon (on iOS/Android launchers that support custom icons) or use Shortcuts to set that saved photo.
